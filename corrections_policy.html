Correction, Review & Removal Policy

  1. Report received: a visitor, organization, or Directory Care review identifies a possible issue.
  2. Listing located: the page, city, category, and resource are identified.
  3. Official-source check: the organization’s website, government notice, public directory, or direct contact information is checked where practical.
  4. Status assigned: Good, Needs Review, Possible Replacement, or Remove.
  5. Change documented: the correction and review date are recorded before the updated site is published.

Chamber of Hope may remove or revise listings that are closed, duplicated, misleading, unsafe, unrelated to the directory’s mission, or no longer publicly accessible. A request for removal is reviewed; it is not automatically granted when the public listing remains accurate and useful.

No invented replacements: A gap may remain temporarily rather than inserting an organization or contact detail that has not been confirmed.
