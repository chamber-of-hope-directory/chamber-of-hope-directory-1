✅ Saginaw — Next Steps Checklist

A quick checklist to move from “need” → “next steps” in 15–30 minutes.

  • Call 2‑1‑1 and ask for the top 3 resources for your exact need + your ZIP code.
  • Apply for benefits via MI Bridges (SNAP/Medicaid/SER), then screenshot your confirmation.
  • If you are in danger or in immediate crisis, call 911. If you need emotional support, call/text 988.
