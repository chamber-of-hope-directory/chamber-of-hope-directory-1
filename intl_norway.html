Quick National Start (Norway): Start with Norge.no, then use the city resource pack below for local routing. Emergency routing: 112 / 113 / 110 / 116 117. For jobs and benefits, use NAV. For immigration and ID support, use UDI and ID-porten.

☎️ National Hotlines, Phones & Start Heres

  • ✅ Police emergency: 112
  • ✅ Ambulance / medical emergency: 113
  • ✅ Fire: 110
  • ✅ Out-of-hours medical service: 116 117
  • ✅ Mental health support: 116 123
  • ✅ Domestic violence helpline: 116 006
  • ✅ Children / youth emergency help: 116 111
  • ✅ Police non-emergency: 02800
  • ✅ Tax Administration: 800 80 000
  • ✅ Norge.no login help: 800 30 300
