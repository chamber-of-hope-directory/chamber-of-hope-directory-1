💝 Giving & Support — Chamber of Hope – Worldwide HELPS Ministry Directory

We are a helps directory ministry—here to point people toward practical help with a Christ-centered heart. We serve all people without discrimination and we are encased in God’s love. We honor the wisdom of our ancestors (Sankofa — “go back and get it”); we worship God alone. Through Christ consciousness, we live from the Kingdom within (Luke 17:21) and seek to do what the Father directs (John 5:19).

“Find help. Share hope. Guided by love, grounded in truth. Hope in your hands. Local links, living faith. Bridges to wholeness.”

💵 Why Give

Flexible one-time or recurring giving helps keep this directory updated, expand to all 50 states, territories, and sustain global outreach. If possible, we ask for $5 or more, but whatever feels right in your heart is welcome. We deeply appreciate every visit to this directory, every share, and every donation. Your generosity is truly appreciated and helps this work continue.
