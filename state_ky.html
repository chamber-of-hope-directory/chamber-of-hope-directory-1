✅ 30. Louisville Next Steps
  1. Start with 211 if you’re unsure: dial 211 or visit kentucky211.org.
  2. Pick your top 2 needs (example: utilities + food) and apply the same day.
  3. Write down your “case notes”: date/time, who you spoke with, confirmation numbers, next steps.
  4. If you hit a wall, call again and ask for a warm transfer to another program.
  5. Safety first: If you’re in danger, call 911 or reach out to 988.
You don’t have to solve everything in one day. One appointment at a time is progress.
