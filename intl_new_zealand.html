  • 🏥 Healthline: 0800 611 116
  • 🧠 Talkline mental health: 1737
  • 🧒 Oranga Tamariki / child concern: 0508 326 459
  • 🏠 Tenancy Services: 0800 836 262
  • 🩹 ACC claims: 0800 101 996
