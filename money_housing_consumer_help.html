Last reviewed: August 6, 2026. Information is educational and connects visitors to official agencies and qualified professionals. Chamber of Hope does not provide legal, medical, tax, insurance, investment, credit-repair, or financial advice and does not promise outcomes.

United States: choose the closest topic below and use the official agency or counselor listed. State, county, city, tribal, and territory rules may differ.

Medicare, Medicaid & Long-Term Care

Find enrollment help, plan comparison, appeals, Medicare Savings Programs, Extra Help, skilled-nursing coverage, Medicaid, and long-term services and supports.

  • Medicare — 1-800-MEDICARE (800-633-4227); TTY 877-486-2048
  • State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) — 877-839-2675
  • Medicaid beneficiary resources and state contacts
  • Medicare long-term-care coverage explanation

Medicare may cover qualifying short-term skilled care, but generally does not pay for ongoing custodial long-term care. Medicaid rules, transfer reviews, estate recovery, exemptions, and hardship waivers vary by state or territory.

Credit, Debt Collection & Consumer Rights

Use official tools to review credit reports, respond to identity theft, understand validation notices, dispute inaccurate information, and report unfair or deceptive practices.

  • AnnualCreditReport.com — 877-322-8228
  • CFPB debt-collection help — 855-411-2372
  • IdentityTheft.gov recovery plan — FTC 877-382-4357
  • Submit a CFPB complaint
  • Find a state attorney general

A dispute or validation request does not automatically erase a valid debt. Accurate negative information generally cannot be removed merely because it is unfavorable. State limitation periods and lawsuit rules differ; contact legal aid or a licensed attorney for legal guidance.
