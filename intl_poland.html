Quick National Start (Poland): Start with Gov.pl and the official tools below. For emergencies use 112. For health routing use NFZ 800 190 590. For domestic violence use Niebieska Linia 800 120 002. For child and youth crisis support, try 116 111.

🚨 Emergency / safety

  • ✅ Emergency number 112 — Free emergency number across Poland and the EU.
  • ✅ Alarm112 mobile application — Official app for sending emergency notifications, especially useful for deaf and hard-of-hearing users.

🩺 Health / patient help

  • ✅ NFZ / Patient Information Line — Phone 800 190 590 (24/7); from abroad +48 22 125 66 00.
  • ✅ mojeIKP — Free patient app for e-prescriptions, referrals, and health account access.

🛡️ Violence / crisis / children

  • ✅ Niebieska Linia / Blue Line — Domestic violence helpline 800 120 002, free 24/7.
  • ✅ Child & youth helpline 116 111 — Support center for children and young people.

⚖️ Rights / immigration / aid

  • ✅ Commissioner for Human Rights — Help line 800 676 676; address al. Solidarności 77, 00-090 Warszawa.
  • ✅ Office for Foreigners — ul. Koszykowa 16, 00-564 Warszawa • contact 47 721 76 75.
  • ✅ Free legal aid — Public free legal aid and citizens advice available in every poviat.

💼 Work / benefits / disability / business

  • ✅ Praca.gov.pl — Public employment services and job search tools.
  • ✅ ePraca — Mobile-friendly job offers, fairs, and training listings.
  • ✅ ZUS — Social insurance, pensions, and benefit information.
  • ✅ PFRON — Disability support and rehabilitation fund services.
  • ✅ Biznes.gov.pl — Official information and services for starting and running a business.
  • ✅ UOKiK consumer rights — Consumer rights help and complaint guidance.
