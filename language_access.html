Help Finding an Interpreter

  • Hospitals/clinics: Ask for “interpreter services” / “language access” — many providers are required to offer interpretation in certain settings.
  • Government/city services: Ask for language access or an interpreter at the public counter; use the city portal links in your hub pages.
  • Community organizations: Swiss Red Cross • Caritas often help connect people to local support.

Emergency Note

If you are in danger: call your local emergency number. In Switzerland: 112 (emergency), 117 (police), 118 (fire), 144 (ambulance), 1414 (REGA).
