✅ 30. Nashville Next Steps
  1. Start with Tennessee 211: visit tn211 or dial 211 for local programs.
  2. Pick your top 2 needs (example: food + rent) and apply the same day.
  3. Apply for benefits (SNAP/childcare/other) via OneDHS Portal.
  4. Keep “case notes”: date/time, who you spoke with, confirmation numbers, next steps.
  5. If you hit a wall, ask for a warm transfer or call back—funding rules change.
  6. Safety first: If you’re in danger, call 911. For crisis support, call/text 988.
You don’t have to solve everything in one day. One appointment at a time is progress.
