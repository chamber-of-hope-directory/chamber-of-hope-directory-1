Shared Resources, Navigation & Contact Guide

Chamber of Hope Navigation helps visitors understand where to click, what belongs in shared resources, and how to contact Chamber of Hope without confusing urgent help, advertising, corrections, resource suggestions, and support requests.

Important: Chamber of Hope shares resource pathways and official starting points. It does not provide emergency response, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, insurance advice, tax advice, counseling, case management, document retrieval, or guaranteed services.

What Goes Where

  • Urgent safety, crisis, storms, disasters, or public health alerts: use Emergency & Safety, Public Health & Food Safety, and Official Alert & Global Help Watch.
  • Help-request scripts, before-you-call checklists, waitlist/denial questions, caregiver notes, and access reminders: use Help Request Tools & Next-Step Checklists.
  • Benefits, insurance, retirement, children, disability, caregivers, and survivor planning: use Life Stages, Benefits & Future Planning.
  • Birth, death, marriage, divorce, adoption, cemetery, military, immigration, property, archive, and family-history records: use Records, Archives & Family History.
  • Country-specific government, pension, benefit, health, legal, refugee, consulate, or disaster starting points: use International & Global Country Guidance.
  • Puerto Rico, USVI, Guam, CNMI, and American Samoa differences: use U.S. Territories Guidance.
  • Large nonprofits, organizations, churches, businesses, and sponsored visibility: use Advertise & Partner With Us, not urgent-help pages.

Navigation Rule for Chamber of Hope

New sections should be linked where visitors naturally look, but the homepage should stay calm. The goal is simple: help visitors choose one clear doorway without turning the directory into a crowded wall of information.
