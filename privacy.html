Privacy Notice

Chamber of Hope is designed to let visitors browse public resource information without creating an account.

Forms

Suggestion, correction, advertising, prayer-request, and other forms may collect the information a visitor voluntarily submits. Form submissions may be processed through website and made available to the site administrator. Do not place sensitive medical, financial, immigration, legal-case, abuse-case, password, Social Security, or banking information in a form.

Saved pages

The “My Help List” feature stores saved page links and personal notes only in the browser’s local storage on the visitor’s device. Chamber of Hope does not receive that saved list through this feature. Clearing browser data may erase it, and anyone with access to the device may be able to view it.

External services

Opening Google Translate, Google Search, Google Maps, ChatGPT, donation services, email, or an outside resource takes the visitor to another service with its own privacy practices. Chamber of Hope does not control those outside services.

Safety

The Quick Exit button leaves for Google, but it cannot guarantee removal of browser history, downloads, autofill, screenshots, network logs, or device monitoring.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to 8chamber8ofhope8@gmail.com.
