Quick Help (Israel)

  • 🚓 Police (Emergency): 100
  • 🚑 Ambulance / Magen David Adom: 101
  • 🚒 Fire & Rescue: 102
  • 🛡️ Home Front Command: 104
  • 🧑🏽‍🤝‍🧑🏼 Welfare / Domestic Violence Hotline: 118
  • 🏥 Ministry of Health “Kol Habriut”: *5400 / 08-624-1010
  • 🧠 ERAN emotional support: 1201
  • 🆘 Sexual assault hotline (women): 1202
  • 🆘 Sexual assault hotline (men): 1203
  • 🏙️ Municipal information line: 106 / 107 (many cities)
  • ⚡ Additional volunteer emergency medical response: 1221 (United Hatzalah)
