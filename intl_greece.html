  • ✅ Gov.gr — Greece’s main digital government portal.
  • ✅ National Emergency Numbers — Official emergency routing including 112, 100, 166, and 199.
  • ✅ myKEPlive — Remote appointments with Citizens’ Service Centres (KEP).

  • ✅ DYPA — Jobs, unemployment benefits, and training.
  • ✅ AADE / IAPR — Taxes, tax residency, and filings.
