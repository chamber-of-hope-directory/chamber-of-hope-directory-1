  1. Pick the urgent need: shelter tonight, food today, safety, or medical.
  2. Call Maine 2‑1‑1 (or text 898‑211) and ask specifically for “Portland / Cumberland County” options.
  3. Write down case numbers + names: keep a simple notebook or notes app.

  5. Ask about free/low‑cost options in every category (clinics, meds, transit, IDs, legal clinics).
