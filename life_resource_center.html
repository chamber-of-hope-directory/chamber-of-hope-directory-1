CHAMBER OF HOPE LIFE RESOURCE CENTER

Life Guides, Safety & Planning Resources

Start here for verified guidance through major life needs and transitions. Each section connects visitors with official agencies, established nonprofits, qualified professionals, and the correct Chamber of Hope directory pathway.

Last reviewed: August 6, 2026. This page is educational and does not provide legal, medical, financial, immigration, tax, veterinary, or other professional advice.

How to Use This Page

Choose the situation closest to your need. United States visitors can use the official national links below and then check state, territory, county, or local programs. Visitors outside the United States should begin with the relevant national ministry, regulator, civil registry, public employment service, emergency agency, or embassy/consulate.

1. Digital Life & Online Safety

Help with hacked accounts, identity theft, phishing, malware, scams, privacy, lost devices, cyberbullying, account recovery, and safer online habits.

  • Use the service provider's official account-recovery page.
  • Change compromised passwords and turn on multi-factor authentication.
  • Report identity theft, fraud, or cybercrime through official channels.
  • Protect children and vulnerable adults from online exploitation.
  • CISA Secure Our World
  • IdentityTheft.gov
  • FTC scam guidance

2. Immigration, Citizenship & International Relocation

Official starting points for immigration pathways, citizenship, visas, passports, refugee or asylum resources, family reunification, travel documents, and relocation.

  • Use only government immigration agencies or accredited representatives.
  • Confirm requirements with the destination country's official authority.
  • Use embassy or consular services for documents and emergencies abroad.
  • U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services
  • U.S. passports, visas and travel
  • International Organization for Migration
  • UNHCR refugee resources

3. Employment & Careers

Career exploration, job search, resumes, interviews, apprenticeships, training, credentials, licensing, career changes, disability employment, veteran employment, and small-business counseling.

  • CareerOneStop — 877-872-5627
  • Apprenticeship.gov
  • American Job Centers
  • International Labour Organization

Chamber of Hope does not guarantee employment, wages, licensing, funding, or program acceptance.

4. Consumer Protection

Help with scams, identity theft, billing disputes, credit reports, debt-collection rights, online shopping, product recalls, banking complaints, contractor fraud, travel problems, and unfair or deceptive practices.

  • FTC Consumer Advice — 877-382-4357

  • State consumer protection offices
  • U.S. product recalls

A complaint or dispute does not guarantee a refund, deletion, settlement, or legal outcome.
