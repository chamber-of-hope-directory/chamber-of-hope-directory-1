How Resources Are Listed

Small labels help you know what kind of page will open. They describe the link type; they do not promise that a program is currently open or accepting requests.

Official

Government or public source

A government department, public agency, court, embassy, public-health portal, or similar public source.

Website

Organization or service website

A website connected to the named organization, program, hotline, learning tool, or service.

Search

Search, map, or locator

A starting point that may show several options. Search results can change and are not individually approved organizations.

Pending Review

Awaiting review

A suggested resource should not be treated as approved until it has been checked.

Sponsored

Paid visibility

Sponsored content is identified and kept separate from ordinary help listings.

Before using a resource

Hours, eligibility, funding, locations, and availability can change. Contact the provider directly before traveling or sharing personal information.

Checkmarks and review dates

A checkmark shows that a link is included as a directory starting point. A review date is shown only where the stated page or information was actually reviewed.
