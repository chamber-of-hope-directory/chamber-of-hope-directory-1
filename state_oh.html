✅ 30. Columbus “Next Steps” Checklist

Use this checklist to move from urgent needs to stability—one step at a time.

  • Call 211 and ask for the top 3 programs in Columbus for your biggest need (shelter/food/utilities).
  • Get benefits lined up: SNAP/Medicaid/cash assistance via Ohio Benefits + County JFS.
  • Stabilize housing: ask for coordinated entry/shelter and rental assistance pathways.
  • Address health needs: locate a clinic (HRSA) and schedule primary care.
  • If legal issues: contact legal aid early (eviction, benefits denial, DV protection orders).
  • Build income: connect to OhioMeansJobs + workforce training; ask about interview clothes/bus passes.
  • Save a simple “go folder”: ID, birth certificate, proof of address, benefit letters, prescriptions.
