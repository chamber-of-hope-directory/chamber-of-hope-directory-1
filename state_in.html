✅ Indianapolis — Next Steps Checklist
  • Start with 211 or Indiana 2‑1‑1. Write down your top 3 needs (food, shelter, bills, health).
  • If you need food today: check Gleaners and Indy Hunger Network.
  • If you need shelter/housing help: start with Wheeler Mission, Horizon House, and CHIP.
  • If you need medical care: call HealthNet or Eskenazi.
  • If you are in danger or in a mental health emergency: call 911 or 988. For DV/SA, contact The Julian Center.
  • Gather key docs (ID, proof of address, income/benefits, lease, bills). Take pictures of paperwork on your phone.
  • When things stabilize, schedule next steps: EmployIndy (jobs), Indiana Legal Services (legal), CICOA (seniors).

✅ Terre Haute — Next Steps Checklist
  • Start with 211: Dial 211 and ask for the exact program you need (utilities, shelter, food, rides, legal aid).
  • Have documents ready: photo ID, proof of address, household income, and any shutoff/eviction notices.
  • Housing crisis: Contact Reach Services Pathways + Catholic Charities shelters early in the day; ask about waitlists and referrals.
  • Food today: Use Catholic Charities Foodbank + Salvation Army + Light House Mission; confirm hours before you travel.
  • Health: WV Health Center can help if you’re uninsured; Vigo County Health Clinic for vaccines/testing/physicals.
  • Keep notes: Write down who you spoke with, dates, and what they requested—this speeds up approvals.
