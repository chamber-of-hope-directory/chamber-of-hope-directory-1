Advertising & Sponsorship Safety Note

Last reviewed: August 3, 2026

Chamber of Hope may include clearly marked advertising, sponsored visibility, or community-service spotlights in some general areas of the directory. These items help support directory growth, but they stay separate from urgent help, crisis support, public-health alerts, emergency-disaster safety information, and ordinary free-help pathways.

What Sponsored Means for Visitors

If a listing or spotlight is paid or sponsored, the page should make that clear with simple wording such as Sponsored, Paid Listing, Advertising, or Sponsored Community Service.

A sponsored listing is not the same as emergency help, government help, official benefits approval, professional advice, or a guaranteed service.

No Endorsement or Guarantee

Paid placement, advertising, sponsorship, partnership consideration, or visibility does not mean Chamber of Hope endorses, certifies, licenses, guarantees, supervises, formally partners with, or professionally approves any business, organization, church, program, product, service, outcome, or provider.

How to Use Sponsored Information Safely

  • Contact the provider directly before using a service.
  • Confirm current cost, hours, location, service area, and eligibility.
  • Ask about licenses, credentials, contracts, refund terms, privacy practices, and policies when appropriate.
  • Be careful with high-pressure claims, guaranteed benefits, fake grants, pay-to-apply schemes, or requests for sensitive personal information.
  • Use emergency services, official agencies, crisis hotlines, or local authorities for urgent needs.

Advertising & Partnership Requests

Large nonprofits, organizations, churches, community programs, family programs, small businesses, and practical local services may ask about advertising or partnership consideration by emailing 8chamber8ofhope8@gmail.com. Advertising fees are not posted publicly; current options are shared by email after review.

Report a Concern

Visitors may report a concern if a sponsored listing appears confusing, outdated, unsafe, misleading, or mixed into urgent-help pathways.
