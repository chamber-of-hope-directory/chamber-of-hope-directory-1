🏝 U.S. Territories Guidance

Territories are U.S.-connected, but benefits, SSI, Medicaid/CHIP, Medicare help programs, records offices, health coverage, insurance regulation, legal aid, public assistance, and disaster pathways may work differently by territory. Use this guide before assuming 50-state rules apply.

✅ U.S. Territories are included

This directory includes Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands (CNMI), and American Samoa. Use the 🏝 Territories button in the Quick Jump bar to jump here anytime.

Quick links: Puerto Rico U.S. Virgin Islands Guam CNMI American Samoa

How many U.S. territories are there?

The United States has 5 permanently inhabited territories: Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands (CNMI), and American Samoa.

🌊 Other U.S. Minor Outlying Islands
Some U.S. territories are uninhabited or do not have permanent civilian populations and therefore do not operate city-based public service infrastructures. If you are seeking information related to one of these areas, please submit a suggestion and Chamber of Hope will research and respond accordingly.

Quick Help (Applies Across U.S. States & Territories)

  • Emergency: Call 911 for immediate danger or medical emergencies.
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call/text 988 • 988lifeline.org
  • Disaster & Recovery (FEMA): fema.gov • apply for assistance, find shelters, recovery centers.
  • American Red Cross: redcross.org • disaster support, shelter info, preparedness.
  • FindHelp (U.S. service locator): findhelp.org • search by ZIP (many areas covered; availability varies).
