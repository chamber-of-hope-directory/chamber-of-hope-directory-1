🚨 Emergency

  • Immediate danger: Call 112 (EU-wide)
  • Police: Call 091
  • Medical emergencies: Call 061
  • Gender violence (support): Call 016
  • Suicidal thoughts / crisis support: Call 024
If you’re traveling in the EU, 112 works across member countries. For non-emergencies, use the Resource Links and local social services portals.
