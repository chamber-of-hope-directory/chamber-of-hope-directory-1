What “Partner With Us” Means

A partnership request is a request to connect with Chamber of Hope for resource sharing, community support, sponsored visibility, awareness outreach, directory support, or a possible collaboration conversation.

Partnership consideration does not mean automatic approval, endorsement, certification, a formal legal partnership, shared responsibility, or a guarantee of placement. Requests are reviewed to make sure they fit the directory’s mission, safety, and community-care purpose.

Advertising & Sponsored Visibility Options

Chamber of Hope reviews advertising and sponsored-visibility requests carefully. Advertising details are shared by email after a request is reviewed because needs may vary by placement, location, section, length, update needs, availability, and what is being requested.

Basic Listing Request

Business, church, organization, or program name, short description, city/service area, public phone if available, and website or social link.

Good for a simple starter presence request.

Featured Visibility Request

A request for stronger visibility inside a clearly marked sponsored area while staying separate from urgent/free help resources.

Good for a service, organization, church, nonprofit, or small business that wants more attention without taking over the page.

City or Section Spotlight Request

A request for sponsored placement connected to one city, one topic area, one country/territory pathway, or one practical-help section if space is available.

Placement depends on fit, availability, review, and whether it supports the directory’s mission and safety standards.

Advertising request note: Please email first to ask about current options and what may be needed. Do not send any payment unless Chamber of Hope reviews the request and replies with next steps.

What Sponsored Means for Visitors

Some listings or spotlight areas may be paid or sponsored. When that happens, Chamber of Hope aims to keep it clear with simple wording such as Sponsored, Paid Listing, Advertising, or Sponsored Community Service.

Sponsored information is not emergency help, government help, official benefit approval, professional advice, endorsement, or a guarantee of service. Visitors should contact the provider directly, ask questions, confirm current details, and make their own informed decision.

Safety Review

Advertising and partnership requests may be reviewed before posting. Requests that do not fit the directory’s mission, safety, family-friendly purpose, or community-care focus may not be included.

  • No misleading “guaranteed benefits,” fake grants, pay-to-apply schemes, or high-pressure claims.
  • No ads pretending to be Chamber of Hope, a government agency, emergency service, or crisis hotline.
  • No unsafe, exploitative, discriminatory, illegal, adult, hate-based, predatory, or unrelated content.
  • No requests for visitors’ sensitive personal information through Chamber of Hope.
  • Advertisers are responsible for keeping their own contact details, service details, licenses, and policies accurate.

No Endorsement / No Advice / No Guarantee

Paid placement, advertising, sponsorship, or visibility does not mean Chamber of Hope endorses, certifies, licenses, guarantees, supervises, formally partners with, or professionally approves any business, organization, church, program, product, service, outcome, or provider.

Chamber of Hope HELPS Directory provides resource pathways and starting points only. It does not provide legal, medical, mental-health, emergency, financial, insurance, tax, investment, benefits, immigration, housing, or professional advice.

Visitors should contact providers directly, confirm current details, ask questions, check licensing or credentials when appropriate, review service terms, contracts, refund policies, and use their own judgment before using any service.

Quick Questions

Can churches or ministries request visibility?

Yes. Churches, ministries, and faith/community programs may ask, especially when they offer practical services such as pantry support, daycare, tutoring, outreach, family programs, youth programs, classes, or community help.

Can large nonprofits or organizations request visibility?

Yes. Large nonprofits, larger organizations, community organizations, and established programs may request advertising, sponsored visibility, or partnership consideration when the information may be useful to visitors and fits the directory’s mission.

Can small businesses request advertising?

Yes. Practical, family-friendly local services may request visibility, including beauty, grooming, home help, food/cake services, auto help, notary, tax preparation, cleaning, moving, lawn care, and other community-useful services.

Can advertising be requested internationally or in U.S. territories?

Possibly, but the request must clearly state the country, territory, city, service area, public contact details, and what visitors need to know. International and territory listings may require extra review because laws, licensing, consumer rules, and contact formats vary.

Does partnership mean Chamber of Hope officially endorses the organization?

No. Partnership consideration means a request to connect, share resources, support the directory, sponsor visibility, or discuss a possible community collaboration. It does not mean endorsement, certification, formal legal partnership, shared responsibility, or guaranteed placement.
