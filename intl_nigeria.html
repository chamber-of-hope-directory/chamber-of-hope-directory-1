🌍 International & Global Country Guidance

Country-safe starting point: Use this country’s official government offices and local agencies first. For benefits, health coverage, records, legal aid, insurance, emergency/disaster instructions, refugee/asylum support, or consular help, check the country’s official ministry, civil registry, court, health agency, disaster agency, legal aid office, insurance regulator, embassy, consulate, or recognized humanitarian organization.

Open International & Global Country Guidance

Do not assume U.S. programs, U.S. benefit names, U.S. document names, or U.S. emergency numbers apply outside the United States unless the person is specifically handling U.S.-based benefits, records, citizenship, or consular matters.

🧭 Retirement, Pension, Disability, Child & Health Coverage Help

Benefit systems are different in every country. For pensions, disability benefits, child/family benefits, survivor benefits, health coverage, insurance, and long-term planning, start with the country’s official government benefit office, social security/pension agency, health ministry, insurance regulator, legal aid, or embassy/consulate if relevant.
